Come in. Sit down. The coffee's good and nobody's in a hurry.

We're in an old brick barn in Crit, a small Saxon village in Transylvania. Half of it is an antique shop full of books, ceramics, painted furniture and odd things that washed up here over the years. The other half is a specialty coffee bar.

Dig around -- you might leave with something. A book, a cup, a piece of furniture. Nothing has a catalogue number. Things find their way to a new home when it's time.

## The Coffee

critcoffeeshop. Specialty coffee made with care, not ceremony.
